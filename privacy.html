Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What Viral-Ops is

Viral-Ops is a private tool operated by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

Viral-Ops uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's channels;
  • read the status of those uploaded videos;
  • read YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channels.

Viral-Ops does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own machine or private server. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

Viral-Ops uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
